# Game of Thrones

---

## Overview of the movie

**Game of Thrones** is an epic fantasy television series created by **David Benioff** and **D. B. Weiss**, based on the novels *A Song of Ice and Fire* by **George R. R. Martin**.  
The show explores themes of **power, loyalty, betrayal, and survival** in the fictional continents of Westeros and Essos.

---

## Plot Summary  

Noble families battle for control of the **Iron Throne**, the seat of power in the Seven Kingdoms.  
Meanwhile, ancient forces rise in the North, threatening all of humanity.  
The series weaves political intrigue, epic battles, and shocking twists that redefine the fantasy genre.
